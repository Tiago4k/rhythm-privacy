Rhythm Privacy Policy

Effective 27 July 2026

Rhythm is a private baby care tracking app for recording baby care events and related caregiver information. This may include feeds, nappies, pumping, sleep, growth measurements, notes, reminders, widgets, and family sharing.

Information you add to Rhythm

Rhythm may store information you enter into the app, including baby profile details, baby care events, notes, measurements, caregiver profile details, notification preferences, import/export data, and optional profile images.

Where your data is stored

Rhythm stores app data on your device. If iCloud is available for your Apple account, Rhythm syncs data through Apple iCloud using CloudKit so your own devices can stay up to date.

If you choose to invite another caregiver, Rhythm uses Apple iCloud sharing so invited caregivers can access the shared baby record and related activity. You control who you invite.

Rhythm does not operate its own account system or server for your baby care data. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, track you across apps or websites, or use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Support and feedback

If you contact us by email or send feedback through TestFlight, we may receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, screenshots, crash details, or diagnostic context supplied by Apple. We use this only to respond to support requests and improve Rhythm.

Notifications

Rhythm may send optional notifications if you grant permission. Notification settings can be changed in the app or in iOS Settings.

Data export and deletion

Rhythm includes tools to export app data. You can delete baby profiles and events in the app. You can also manage or remove app data through your device and iCloud settings.

Children’s privacy

Rhythm is intended for use by parents and caregivers. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information directly from children.

Medical disclaimer

Rhythm is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Caregivers should contact a qualified health professional for medical concerns.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the effective date above.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact rhythm.baby.support@gmail.com.
