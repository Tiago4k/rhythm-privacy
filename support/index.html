Help for Rhythm Baby Tracker on iPhone and iPad

Need a hand? Email rhythm.baby.support@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you. It helps to include your device, iOS version and what you were trying to do.

Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. Open Rhythm, add your baby and start logging. Your log is stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud account if iCloud is turned on.

How do I open Settings?

On the Today screen, tap the profile pill at the top to open Settings.

iCloud sync and sharing

My entries aren’t showing on my other device

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud Drive is on, and that Rhythm is allowed to use iCloud in iOS Settings. Pull down on Today to refresh.

How do I share with another caregiver?

Go to Settings, then Family members, and choose Invite family. Rhythm uses iCloud sharing, so the person you invite needs an Apple Account signed in to iCloud. Everyone you invite sees the same log.

How many caregivers can I invite?

Rhythm includes one invited caregiver for free. Rhythm Plus lets you invite more trusted caregivers.

Rhythm Plus

What does Rhythm Plus include?

Predictions on Today, Advanced Trends with longer and custom ranges, prediction widgets and Lock Screen views, smart reminders and more trusted caregivers. Manual logging, timers, CSV export, multiple babies, basic Trends and one invited caregiver stay free.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Settings, tap Rhythm Plus, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings, tap Rhythm Plus, then Manage subscription, or go to iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How do I export my data?

Open Settings, tap Privacy & data, then Export data (CSV).

How do I delete data?

You can delete individual entries from the Journal, or delete a baby from Settings. Deleting the app removes data from your device; data synced to iCloud can be managed in iOS Settings under your Apple Account and iCloud.
