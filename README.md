# rhythm-privacy

Public privacy policy and support pages for Rhythm, the baby tracker on the App Store (UK and US).

Published with GitHub Pages at https://tiago4k.github.io/rhythm-privacy/

- `index.html`: privacy policy, linked from the App Store listing and the app's Settings.
- `support/index.html`: support page, used as the App Store Support URL.

Keep both pages in line with the shipped app and its App Store privacy answers. App work and open issues live in [Tiago4k/Rhythm](https://github.com/Tiago4k/Rhythm/issues).
